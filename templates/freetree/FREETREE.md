# FREETREE

FREETREE is an isolated open-development clone of Mirror-DataNest.

## Repository-development contract

- No automatic synchronization to DataNest or Mirror-DataNest.
- No canonical production authority.
- No DataNest promotion, certification, or repository-boundary gates.
- No Knowledge ingestion or feed exchange.
- No Boundaries assessment.
- Development may proceed directly on FREETREE according to repository permissions.
- Platform security, credential hygiene, and applicable law remain in force.
- Scheduled maintenance removes only redundant branches with no unique commits and applies deterministic safe text streamlining.

FREETREE is a development sandbox, not a route around canonical production authorization.
